The 5 → 162 spike is relabeling Reporting logic
Deals in the Still Employed stage by creation week, as the current report counts them. JLG's Failed to Qualify – Still Employed workflow (190586263) was broken from mid-August until 10/16.

Why it broke. "Bucket = Bad" was an exit goal, and another workflow set it right after survey submission, so contacts exited before the 5-minute delay. Intake moved these deals by hand until about Aug 17, which hid the problem.

Why it spiked. Every survey submission creates a deal. Once fixed on 10/16, the workflow moved every "Yes" lead to Still Employed, including people who never booked. The report counts that stage as SE bookings, so it shows 5 → 162 and an inflated 92% lead-to-book.

The cancellation email doesn't cancel. The meeting stays on the calendar, and the email includes a link to reschedule.

Corrected Still Employed booking logic
CS Booked + SE stage = 87
Count a lead only when it hit both stages, not any lead that reached Still Employed.
How DQ'd leads still reach the calendar Side doors
The survey works. A "Yes" goes to the "we can't help you" page with no calendar, on both LP versions. The LP test isn't the cause.

Retaking the survey. They answer Yes, see the DQ page, go back and answer No, then get the calendar and our emails. About 9 a week, the biggest source today.

Ads straight to the calendar. CORE | SCHEDULE and one other campaign link directly to the booking page, so there's no survey. Booking widget to confirm (MS, JJ).

JLG's own site. Leads leave our page and book on jlglawyers.com or JLG's Instagram bio link, which have no survey.

Mobile calendar. /booking showed JLG's calendar on mobile, which asked again and booked leads even on Yes. Fixed today.

Our follow-up emails. The "filled form but didn't book" sequence sent DQ'd leads a booking link: 57 bookings in September. Fixed Oct 5.

Why quality is still down Team discussion
Signals to Meta.

We can only send Opportunity or Customer. We use Opportunity, which beat Customer on downstream results side by side, but it sends every type of lead back to Meta.

We need stronger Lifecycle Stages to improve the signals Meta optimizes on. This needs further team discussion.
